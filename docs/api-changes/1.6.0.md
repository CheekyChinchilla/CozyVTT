# API changes in 1.6.0

What a program driving CozyVTT may notice in CozyVTT 1.6.0, moved word for word from the changelog's "For scripts and integrations" entry. The HTTP routes and live-table events are not a versioned public API, but programs do drive them with a signed-in account. These are the changes such a program may notice; the entries below explain each one for everyone else.

- *Assets.* `GET /api/assets` (with or without `campaignId`, and with `usable=true`) leaves out, for a member of a campaign who is not its DM, a `CAMPAIGN` asset of that campaign that one of its maps names as `imageUrl`, `baseLayerUrl` or `spiritLayerUrl`, and every `MAP` asset of the campaign, unless they uploaded it or one of these shows it to them: the campaign's current map (as its image or base layer, or as its spirit layer while the spirit plane is open to them); the current map of another campaign they are a member of, played in or run, with the same spirit rule, while the asset's uploader is a member of that campaign; a plain text or Markdown document that the campaign's current DM uploaded, written or uploaded in the asset's campaign or shared with it from their own library or the global one (a document of another campaign shared with it does not count), whose first 1 MiB names the asset (`/api/assets/<kind>/<id>`, among its first 200 such addresses), as read when the document was uploaded with `POST /api/assets/upload`, written with `POST /api/assets/documents` or saved with `PUT /api/assets/documents/:id/content`; a file changed on the server's disk by other means is not read again. `GET /api/assets/:id`, `/:id/download`, `/maps/:id`, `/maps/:id/thumbnail`, `/tokens/:id` and `/tokens/:id/thumbnail`, and `PATCH /api/assets/:id/scope`, answer 404 for such an asset, as for an id that names nothing. `DELETE /api/assets/:id` answers 404 "Asset not found" to a member who may not delete it; it was 403 "Only the uploader while a member of the campaign, its DM, or an admin can delete campaign assets". A `tokenImageUrl` naming such an asset in `POST /api/characters` or `PUT /api/characters/:id` answers 403 "You do not have access to that image"; it was accepted. So does an image address naming one in the map, token, token template and creature routes for a DM of another campaign who is a player in the asset's, unless their campaign already stores that address. A member's own `USER` asset that a map names as its spirit layer is listed to them again. A platform admin is listed and served everything, as before, except with `usable=true` and no `campaignId`, which holds them to the member's rule. `DELETE /api/assets/:id` without `force=true` also answers 409 `ASSET_IN_USE` while a plain text or Markdown document names the asset by its address in its first 1 MiB, as read when the document was uploaded, written or saved, and its uploader can read the asset now; only documents of the asset's uploader and of its campaign's members are counted. Each such document is a `usage` entry of the new kind `document`, named with its campaign where it was written in one, for a caller whose document it is, or who runs the campaign it was written in or shared with, or when it is global; the others are folded into one entry with `name` null, and those past the first 100 named are counted in `omitted`. The asset tag `cozyvtt-drawn-map` means a picture made by the new **Mapmaker**. A campaign `MAP` asset carrying it, exactly and in that case, is taken for one: the DM is offered **Mapmaker** on a map using it, and a save of that map writes over it in place with `PUT /api/assets/:id/picture` (below). `POST /api/assets/upload` refuses the tag, in any case, on anything but a `MAP` of `CAMPAIGN` scope, with 400 "The tag cozyvtt-drawn-map is kept for maps drawn in CozyVTT."; a campaign archive's import leaves it out of each asset's tags. A program uploading map pictures should leave it off. `drawn-map` is an ordinary tag.
- *Assets.* New: `PUT /api/assets/:id/picture`, a multipart upload of one `file`, writes it over the file of a `MAP` asset of `CAMPAIGN` scope tagged `cozyvtt-drawn-map`, keeping its id, and answers 200 `{ message, asset, imageUrl }`, where `imageUrl` is `/api/assets/maps/<id>?v=<time in ms>`, new on every save, for the map's `PUT .../maps/:id`; it changes no map itself. Only the DM of the asset's campaign may call it: anyone else who may read the asset, a platform admin included, gets 403 "Only the campaign's DM can save over its drawn pictures", and anyone who may not, 404 "Asset not found". Any other asset answers 400 "Only a picture drawn in CozyVTT can be saved over. Upload a new picture instead.". The file is checked as a map upload is (`MAX_MAP_SIZE_MB`, its type from its bytes, the picture limits) and must be of the format it replaces, or 400 "The new picture must be a PNG file, as the picture it replaces is."; a refused save leaves the picture as it was. `fileSize`, `mimeType` and the thumbnail follow the new file. It counts against the upload rate limit (`ASSET_UPLOAD_RATE_LIMIT`).
- *Assets.* `GET /api/assets/maps/:id` and `/maps/:id/thumbnail` serve a picture tagged `cozyvtt-drawn-map` with `Cache-Control: private, no-cache` and the ETag `"<id>-<size>-<modified ms>"` (`"<id>-thumbnail-<size>-<modified ms>"` for the thumbnail), as an editable text document is served, since a save in Mapmaker writes over it in place. Every other map and token picture is still cached as `private, max-age=31536000, immutable` with the ETag `"<id>"`.
- *Assets.* `DELETE /api/assets/:id` and `PATCH /api/assets/:id/scope`, for a `CAMPAIGN` asset whose uploader is a member who does not run the campaign: while anything uses it other than their own characters, the tokens they control (`controlledBy`) and the documents they wrote, they answer the uploader 403, "Others in the campaign use this asset, so only the campaign's DM can delete it" or "Others in the campaign use this asset, so only the campaign's DM can move it out of the campaign", with or without `force=true`. When the only such use is a character template, which everyone on the instance is offered, the uploader's own included, they answer "A character template uses this asset, so only the campaign's DM can delete it" or "A character template uses this asset, so only the campaign's DM can move it out of the campaign". The campaign's DM and an admin are answered as before.
- *Maps.* A map's `annotations` is an empty array for everyone, the DM included, in `GET /api/campaigns/:campaignId/maps/:id`, the reply to `PUT .../maps/:id/tokens/:tokenId` and `map.changed`. It carried whatever a campaign import had stored from its archive. Nothing in the app reads it. `GET /api/campaigns/:campaignId/maps` gives each map its `campaignId` and `baseLayerUrl` as well, the fields `GET /api/campaigns/:campaignId` already gave each of its maps. `GET /api/campaigns/:campaignId/maps/:id/drawings` lists a map's drawings as the caller may be sent them: every one for the DM, those for everyone for anyone else, and 404 for a map the caller may not read. `PUT /api/campaigns/:campaignId/maps/:id` changing `width`, `height` or `gridSize` scales every drawing on the map with its walls and lights: each point by the change on its axis, rounded to whole pixels and kept within 500 squares of the map's edges, and each width by the mean of the two changes, kept to 1 to 64. Every member who may read the map is then sent `drawings:state`, as they are for the map on screen after a role change or a DM handover. `POST .../maps` and `PUT .../maps/:id` take a map picture's address with a version, `/api/assets/maps/<id>?v=<digits>` (up to 16 digits), as `imageUrl` or `spiritLayerUrl`, and store it as given; it names the same asset, for who may see it, the asset's uses and the Universal VTT export. Any other query after an asset's address still answers 403. A campaign archive names the picture by its id, so an imported map names its copy without a version. `POST .../maps` takes `drawnBackground`, the colour a map drawn in CozyVTT is made in, written `#rrggbb`; anything else, or none, stores `null`. Every map carries it, in each route and event that sends a map, and nothing changes it after.
- *Live table: limits.* A fog `reveal` or `hide` names at most 250,000 cells, the most grid squares any map has. A longer one is refused and nothing is stored: `fog:operation` answers `error` and `POST /api/campaigns/:campaignId/maps/:id/fog/operation` answers 400, both with "A fog operation can name at most 250,000 cells. Use reveal_all or hide_all for the whole map." The list had no maximum. A `map.ping` whose `x` or `y` is more than 250,000 pixels from the map's corner either way, the bound on walls and lights, is dropped with no answer, as a ping that is not a number already was, and it does not count against the sender's 10 pings every ten seconds. Any finite number used to be passed on to the table. `drawing:add` takes 20 a second and 300 a minute from one user, `drawing:remove` 20 a second, and `drawing:clear_mine` and `drawing:clear` 5 every ten seconds each; past them, one sent with an acknowledgement is refused through it each time, and one sent without is told once, with an `error` starting "Too many". `drawing:preview` frames past 60 a second are dropped with no answer. A map's markup layer has two shares, each holding 1,000 drawings and 100,000 points between them: the DM's, every drawing for the DM alone, whoever wrote it, and those for everyone whose author is the campaign's DM as an add is counted, and the players', every other drawing for everyone; an add past its share is answered "The players' share of this map's drawing is full. Erase some first.", or for the DM "Your share of this map's drawing is full. Erase some first." `exploration:reveal` takes 65 a second from one user, all their connections together (it was 40), so a sight of a 500 by 500 map, which the web client sends in 13 reports, from four tabs at once is taken whole; past it a report is dropped with no answer.
- *Live table: events.* The DM's `exploration:reveal` with no `userId`, or with their own, is dropped with no answer and stores nothing; it stored the DM's own explored memory of any map of the campaign, which the web client never sends. Naming a player is as before.
- *Live table: limits.* `ping` is answered at most 10 times a second per account; past that it gets one `error`, "Too many connection checks (ping) at once. Wait a moment, then try again.", and no `pong`. One connection may send at most 500 events a second of every kind together, events no handler takes included: past that an event is dropped before it is read, one sent with an acknowledgement is not answered, and the connection gets one `error` starting "Too many messages at once from this page" each ten seconds. A connection whose client has stopped reading is closed once more than 10,000 packets, or 16 MiB of them, wait to be sent to it, or once its account has more than 32 MiB waiting over all its connections. A `fog:updated`, `fog:cells`, `walls:replaced`, `lights:replaced`, `drawings:state` or `exploration:state` still waiting, with only such events after it, is dropped when a newer one for the same map (and member) is sent. `authenticate` and the requests a page loads a map with are exempt from the 500 a second. A player's `wall:add`, `wall:remove`, `walls:replace`, `light:add`, `light:remove`, `light:update` and `lights:replace` count against the 40 wall and light edits a second before they are refused with "Only DMs can …", so past that they get no answer, or "Too many wall and light edits at once. Wait a moment, then try again." through an acknowledgement.
- *Tokens.* `rotation` in `PUT /api/campaigns/:campaignId/maps/:id/tokens/:tokenId` follows the move rule: while the session is paused or ended (campaign status `PAUSED` or `INACTIVE`), a player's request carrying `rotation` answers 403 with "Players cannot move tokens while the session is paused or ended", the message a `position` change already got, and nothing in it is stored. It used to be stored. The DM's goes through at any time, and a player's `conditions` alone still do. A `token.move.end` for a token deleted while the drop was being stored answers `error` "Token not found"; `token.moved` used to be sent for it. A token's picture must be an asset uploaded as a token picture (type `TOKEN`): `imageUrl` in `POST /api/campaigns/:campaignId/maps/:id/tokens` and `PUT .../maps/:id/tokens/:tokenId`, in `POST /api/campaigns/:campaignId/token-templates`, `PUT .../token-templates/:id`, `POST .../token-templates/from-token` and `POST .../token-templates/:id/copy-to/:targetCampaignId`, and in `POST /api/campaigns/:campaignId/creatures` and `PUT .../creatures/:creatureId`, the creature copied by `POST .../creatures/:creatureId/duplicate`, and `tokenImageUrl` in `POST /api/characters`, `PUT /api/characters/:id`, `POST /api/character-templates` and `PUT /api/character-templates/:id`, naming an existing asset of any other type answer 400 with "The token image must be a token picture from the asset library.", whatever folder the address gives. They were accepted. The address a record already stores may be sent back unchanged whatever it names; a template copied to another campaign is held to the rule.
- *Live table: maps.* Once the table leaves a map, players stop being sent its events. `map.changed` only ever carries the campaign's current map: a resend of a map the table has left since is sent to nobody, the DM included. A drag's `token.moved` frames for a map that is no longer current go to the DM's sockets only (the audience of a drag was kept for up to a second), and a carrier letting go afterwards sends players no `token.moved` or `token:disappeared` for it. `exploration:state` with a member's explored memory reaches their own sockets only for the current map, and still reaches a DM socket previewing them for any map. A DM's `exploration:reveal` naming another member's `userId` on a map that is not current is dropped without an answer and stores nothing. `DELETE /api/campaigns/:campaignId/maps/:id` now sends the DM's sockets a new event, `map.deleted`, with the map's `mapId`; players are sent nothing.
- *Walls and lights.* `wall:add`, `walls:replace` and `light:add` take an acknowledgement function as their last argument and answer it once: `{ ok: true }` once stored, or `{ ok: false, error }` with the refusal, such as "Maximum 5000 wall segments per map" or "Maximum 200 light sources per map", and then send no `error` event. One past the 40 a second edit ceiling is answered `{ ok: false, error: "Too many wall and light edits at once. Wait a moment, then try again." }`; sent without an acknowledgement it is still dropped with no answer, and any other refusal is still an `error` event.
- *Live table: events.* `chat.message` takes `recipientId`, the user id of a member of the campaign, to whisper to them. The message is stored as the new type `WHISPER`, whatever `type` was sent, and the `chat.message` it sends carries `recipientId` and `recipientName`; it goes to the sender's and the recipient's connections to that campaign and to nobody else, the DM included unless they are one of the two. A whisper to yourself is answered "You cannot whisper to yourself", and one to anyone who is not a member, or with a `recipientId` that is not a user id, "That person is not in this campaign"; nothing is stored. A whisper counts against the chat cooldown and the 50 messages a second and 300 a minute as any message does. `type: "WHISPER"` is refused as before, with "Invalid message type. Must be PLAYER or DM.". Six new events draw on a map: `drawing:add`, `drawing:remove`, `drawing:clear_mine`, `drawing:clear` (the DM's alone), `drawing:preview` and `drawings:request`, which the server answers with `drawing:added`, `drawing:removed`, `drawings:cleared`, `drawing:previewed` and `drawings:state`. The DM may draw at any time; a player while **Players may draw** is on, except while the session is paused or has ended; a spectator never. A player erases only their own drawings for everyone. `drawing:clear` takes an optional `authorId`, a member's user id, to erase only that member's drawings for everyone (every drawing of the DM's own when it names the DM), and `drawings:cleared` then carries it; an `authorId` that is not a user id is answered "authorId must be a member's id". A drawing whose `audience` is `DM` is sent to the DM's sockets alone, on every path. What a drawing takes and every refusal are in the WebSocket guide, under **Drawings on the map**. Each combatant in `initiative.state` carries `characterId`, its token's character or `null`, to each member only where the map sends them that token: `null` on an obscured combatant for everyone but its controller and the DM, `null` on a lit map for a combatant out of a player's sight and for every one to a spectator, and kept on the DM's entry for a token since deleted. `campaign.access.revoked` has a fourth `reason`, `left`, with the message "You left this campaign.", sent to each connection a member who leaves has open to that campaign. A removal and a leave are followed by `presence.state` to the campaign. A token a member is carrying when they are removed from the campaign or leave it, or when their connection authenticates into another campaign, is sent back to its square as on a disconnect: `token.moved` with `movedBy: null`, and `token:appeared` or `token:disappeared` where a frame had carried it in or out of a player's sight. A `token.move.start` or `token.move` still being checked when its connection authenticates into another campaign is sent to nobody in that campaign.
- *Chat history.* `GET /api/campaigns/:campaignId/messages` gives each message `recipientId`, `recipientName` and `recipient`, which are `null` on a message to everyone, and leaves out whispers between other people, for the DM too. A whisper whose recipient's account was deleted is returned to its sender alone.
- *Live table: characters.* A new event, `character.hp.temporary`, takes `characterId` and `temporary`, a whole number from 1 to 99,999 (otherwise `error` "temporary must be a whole number of hit points from 1 to 99,999"), and gives a D&D 5e or Pathfinder 2e character that many temporary hit points in place of any it has; it acts for the same people on the same characters as `character.hp.update`, sends `character.hp.updated` as it does, takes 20 a second from one user, and answers "Call of Cthulhu sheets keep no temporary hit points" for a Call of Cthulhu character.
- *Requests.* A request holding U+0000, the null character, anywhere in its JSON body, a key included, or as `%00` in its path or query string answers 400 with `{ "error": "Validation Error", "message": "Text cannot contain the null character (U+0000)." }`, whatever the route, before the sign-in is checked, and nothing is stored. So does one in a form field or the file's name on `POST /api/assets/upload`, `POST /api/campaigns/import` and `POST /api/campaigns/:campaignId/maps/import-uvtt`, which keep nothing of the upload. Passwords are exempt and taken as they are sent, as before: `password` on `POST /api/auth/login`, `/api/auth/register`, `/api/setup/init`, `/api/auth/mfa/setup`, `/api/auth/mfa/disable`, `/api/auth/mfa/backup-codes` and `DELETE /api/auth/account`, `newPassword` on `POST /api/auth/reset-password`, `currentPassword` and `newPassword` on `POST /api/auth/change-password`, and `currentPassword` on `PUT /api/users/:id`, each as text at the top of its own route's body only. Where the character reached the database the answer was 500; a display name or a drawing's text label had it removed, and a document's text was refused with a message of its own. A live-table event holding it is refused with the same message, through its acknowledgement every time when it has one, and otherwise as `error` once in ten seconds for the connection, as past a flood ceiling; `chat.message` answered "Failed to send chat message" and `dice.roll` "Failed to roll dice". A campaign archive's data files are read with U+0000 taken out of every text and key, and the import's `warnings` gives one line a file, such as "campaign.json: removed 2 null characters (U+0000) from its text, which the app cannot store."; such an archive was refused with 500 "The campaign could not be saved, so nothing was imported. The server log has the details."
- *Campaigns.* `PUT /api/campaigns/:campaignId` takes `playersMayDraw`, `true` or `false` (anything else answers 400 with "playersMayDraw must be true or false"), and the campaign carries it, `true` for every campaign until a DM turns it off. `campaign.settings.updated` carries it as well, and is sent when an update includes it. A campaign archive's `campaign.json` carries it; an archive without it imports with `true`. `POST /api/campaigns/:campaignId/leave`, with no body, takes the signed-in member out of the campaign as the DM's `DELETE /api/campaigns/:campaignId/members/:userId` does, and answers 200 with `{ "message": "You left this campaign." }` for a player or a spectator, 401 when not signed in and 403 "You are not a member of this campaign" for anyone else. The campaign's owner is answered 409 "You own this campaign, so you cannot leave it. If you no longer want it, delete it in the campaign's settings.", whatever their role, and the DM 409 "You run this campaign. Hand the DM seat to someone else in the campaign's settings, then leave it."
- *Campaign archives.* A map file carries the map's drawings under a new key, `drawings`, in drawing order, each with `audience`, `layer`, `kind`, `color`, `fillColor`, `width`, `rotation`, `sortKey`, `points`, `text` and `share`, `DM` for every drawing for the DM alone and the campaign's DM's own, and `PLAYERS` for every other. Its `annotations`, which carried whatever an earlier import had stored and which nothing drew, is written empty: releases up to 1.5.4 store an archive's annotations and send them to every member, and leave out a key they do not know, so an archive from this release imported there carries no drawing to its players. An import takes each entry of `drawings`, and of an older archive's `annotations`, as `drawing:add` would, with a new id, the DM's share as the importing DM's and the players' with no author (an entry for the DM alone goes to the DM's share whatever it names, and one for everyone without `share` to the players'), and leaves out and counts in its warnings any it cannot store, any more than 500 squares outside the map, and any past the 1,000 drawings and 100,000 points each share holds; the map imports either way. Map files are written without indentation, about a third of the size: a map holding all the drawing it may, a full fog and 5,000 walls makes a file of about 5 MB of the 10 MB one may hold, with room for about 800 tokens carrying the longest stat block in the SRD. Each entry of `assets/asset-manifest.json` for an asset of the campaign's own library (`CAMPAIGN` scope, this campaign), or of the exporting user's own (`USER` scope, uploaded by them), carries the asset's `name`, text, `description`, text or `null`, and `tags`, a list of text; one for another member's personal asset, or a global one, that the campaign uses carries none of the three, as before. An import gives each asset the name and description its entry has, trimmed, with one empty or not text taken as none; a name over 200 characters or a description over 1,000 is cut to fit and its `warnings` says so, as `Asset "<file name>": shortened its name to 200 characters.` An asset with no name, as from an archive made by an earlier version, is named after its file without the extension, as before, and one with no description has none. It gives each asset the tags its entry has, tidied as `POST /api/assets/upload` tidies them: each trimmed and split at commas, with empty ones and repeats dropped. A tag that is not text or is over 50 characters is left out, and of the rest the first 50 are kept, in order. A `tags` of more than 1,000 entries, or one that is not a list, imports as none. An entry without `tags` imports with none, as before. A map file carries the map's `drawnBackground`; one without it, or with anything but a colour written `#rrggbb`, imports with `null`. A token, creature or token template whose picture names an asset the archive gives a type other than `TOKEN` imports without its picture, which is still imported as what it was, and `warnings` says so, as for a map's picture: `Map "<name>": left out the pictures of 2 tokens, which were not uploaded as token pictures.`, `Creature "<name>": left out its picture, which was not uploaded as a token picture.`, and the same for a token template. It used to keep it.
- *Saved rolls.* `POST /api/campaigns/:campaignId/macros` and `PUT /api/campaigns/:campaignId/macros/:macroId` take `characterName` and `purpose`, each text of at most 200 characters after trimming, or `null`. On a `POST`, one left out, empty or `null` is stored as `null`. On a `PUT`, one sent empty or `null` is cleared, one left out is kept, and either alone is an edit. A longer one answers 400 with "Character name must be 200 characters or fewer" or "Purpose must be 200 characters or fewer", and one that is not text "Character name must be text" or "Purpose must be text". Every saved roll the macro routes return carries both, from `GET /api/campaigns/:campaignId/macros`, `GET /api/campaigns/:campaignId/macros/:macroId`, `POST` and `PUT`, as do those listed by `GET /api/campaigns/left` and `GET /api/campaigns/left/:campaignId`. A `PUT` with none of the four fields answers 400 with "Nothing to update: send a name, an expression, a character name or a purpose" (it was "Nothing to update: send a name, an expression, or both").
- *Backups.* `POST /api/admin/backups` answers 201 with `warning`, a message, beside `filename`, `sizeBytes` and `createdAt` when the backup is larger than the Admin Panel's restore takes (4 GB, 100,000 files, or 10 GiB unpacked); the backup is made and kept, and the activity log records it at `WARNING`. Without such a backup the answer is as before.
