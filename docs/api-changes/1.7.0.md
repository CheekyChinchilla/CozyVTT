# API changes in 1.7.0

What a program driving CozyVTT may notice in CozyVTT 1.7.0. The HTTP routes and live-table events are not a versioned public API, but programs do drive them with a signed-in account. These are the changes such a program may notice; the changelog's entries explain each one for everyone else.

- *Accounts and requests.* A user's `lastLoginAt` is now set when a sign-in completes: by `POST /api/auth/login` without two-factor authentication, `POST /api/auth/mfa/verify-login` with it, `POST /api/auth/register` when it signs the person in, and `POST /api/setup/init`. Registration and the setup wizard left it null, and `POST /api/auth/login` set it at the password step, including for a sign-in that then stopped at the code step or was refused with 403 `Account Pending Approval`. `POST /api/admin/users/:id/resend-invite` therefore answers 409 for an account that ran the setup wizard or registered and was signed in straight away (it answered 200), and 200 for an account whose only sign-in attempts were refused with 403 `Account Pending Approval` or stopped at the code step (it answered 409), since those no longer set `lastLoginAt`; and `POST /api/auth/reset-password` with an invitation link no longer restarts such an administrator's 7 days to set up two-factor authentication. `GET /api/auth/me` from a signed-in session sets a `lastLoginAt` that is still null, once, and the `user` it answers with carries the new value.
- *Accounts and requests.* The `RateLimit-*` headers on `POST /api/auth/mfa/verify-login` answers are now always the per-address limit's (5 wrong codes per 15 minutes from the address, `RateLimit-Policy: 5;w=900`), as on `POST /api/auth/login`. The per-account limit's replaced them, so `RateLimit-Remaining` counted the account's wrong codes from every address. The 429 `Too many MFA login attempts for this account, please try again later` keeps its `Retry-After`, and its `RateLimit-*` headers are now the address's.
- *Accounts and requests.* `PUT /api/users/:id` now caps changing your own email address at 5 attempts an hour per account, from every sign-in and address. A request counts when it reaches the cap's check, which comes after the `displayName` and `avatarUrl` checks and after `email` is found to be a valid address other than your current one in any letter case: with the right `currentPassword`, a wrong one or none, to an address another account has (still answered 400 "Email already in use") or a free one, and one that makes the change. A request refused before that check does not count: 400 "Invalid email format", a refused `displayName` or `avatarUrl`, or a 429 from the wrong-password limit or the limit on password checks at once from one address, which run first when the body has a `currentPassword`. Past the cap it answers 429 `{ "error": "Rate Limited", "message": "You have tried to change your email address 5 times in the last hour, the most this server allows. Please try again later." }` with `Retry-After` (seconds until the oldest counted attempt is an hour old) and no `RateLimit-*` headers of its own, before the password is checked; the refusal is not counted. A request that changes only the display name, avatar or bio, or sends the current address in any letter case, does not count and is not held, and an admin changing someone else's address is not capped.
- *Accounts and requests.* `DELETE /api/auth/account` from someone who runs a campaign answers 409 "You run "<name>". Hand the GM seat to someone else in the campaign's settings, or delete the campaign, then delete your account.", and `DELETE /api/users/:id` for such a user answers 409 "This user runs "<name>". Hand each one's GM seat to another member, or delete it, then delete the user." Both said `DM seat`.

- *Assets.* `DELETE /api/assets/:id` (with or without `force=true`) and `PATCH /api/assets/:id/scope` (a move out of the campaign) by a campaign asset's uploader who does not run the campaign now first read up to 100 of the campaign's other members' documents stored before 1.6.0 (a PDF among them, which names nothing) and not yet read for the assets they name. While any is still unread (more than 100 were waiting, another request is reading it, or its file could not be read), the delete answers 403 "Some older documents have not yet been checked for the pictures they show, so for now only the campaign's GM can delete this asset. Try again later." and the move 403 "Some older documents have not yet been checked for the pictures they show, so for now only the campaign's GM can move this asset out of the campaign. Try again later."; each request reads up to 100 more, newest first. A document whose file cannot be read is tried again by every request and keeps the refusal until it can be read; until then only the campaign's GM can delete or move the asset. A document read this way that shows the asset now gives the existing 403 "Others in the campaign use this asset, so only the campaign's GM can delete it" (the move: "Others in the campaign use this asset, so only the campaign's GM can move it out of the campaign"); before, until the document had been read, the delete answered 200 with `force=true` and 200 or 409 `ASSET_IN_USE` without it, and the move answered 200. The campaign's GM and admins are answered as before. The "Others in the campaign use this asset" pair said `DM` where it now says GM; the two "Some older documents" messages are new in this release.
- *Assets.* A token picture uploaded from the web client's editor for a `HORROR_D100` sheet with no name is sent to `POST /api/assets/upload` with `name` "Character Token" (it was "Investigator Token").
- *Assets.* `POST /api/assets/upload`, `POST /api/assets/documents` and `PATCH /api/assets/:id/scope` refuse a campaign-scope asset from a spectator, or from a player for anything but token art, with 403 "Only the GM can upload campaign assets"; the message gave the role's full name.
- *Assets.* Refusals that said `DM` now say GM, with the same status codes: `DELETE /api/assets/:id` answers 403 "Only the uploader while a member of the campaign, its GM, or an admin can delete campaign assets", "Others in the campaign use this asset, so only the campaign's GM can delete it" and "A character template uses this asset, so only the campaign's GM can delete it"; `PATCH /api/assets/:id/scope` answers 403 "Only the asset owner or campaign GM can move this asset", "Others in the campaign use this asset, so only the campaign's GM can move it out of the campaign" and "A character template uses this asset, so only the campaign's GM can move it out of the campaign"; `PUT /api/assets/:id/picture` answers 403 "Only the campaign's GM can save over its drawn pictures". Each said `DM` where it now says GM.
- *Backups.* `POST /api/admin/backups/restore` now converts the restored database's `HORROR_D100` characters and character templates from the shape before 1.7.0 to the rebuilt sheet after its migrations run, before it answers. When that conversion fails it answers 500 `{ "error": "Restore Incomplete" }` with the message "The backup was restored and brought up to this version, but converting its Horror d100 character sheets to the rebuilt sheet failed. Restart the backend (docker compose restart backend), which converts them on start, then check the server logs. ", then the note on the uploaded files if copying them failed too, then the line naming the safety backup, and the activity log's entry for the restore carries `sheetConversionFailed: true`. When the conversion runs but some sheets cannot be converted (each named in the server log and left as it was), it answers 500 `{ "error": "Restore Incomplete" }` with the message "The backup was restored and brought up to this version, but N of its Horror d100 character sheets could not be converted to the rebuilt sheet; the server log names each. They are still shown in the rebuilt shape, and the backend tries them again each time it starts: restart it (docker compose restart backend) and check the server logs. " ("1 of its Horror d100 character sheets" for one), then the same file note and safety-backup line, and the activity log's entry carries `sheetsNotConverted` with the count.

- *Campaign archives.* `POST /api/campaigns/import/preview`, `POST /api/campaigns/import` and `POST /api/admin/backups/restore` read an uploaded archive's directory with another reader. It finds the end-of-directory record wherever a record with a comment of up to 65,535 bytes can start, where the old one looked only in the archive's last 80 bytes, and it requires that record and its comment to end the file. Every archive a CozyVTT version wrote is answered as before, and the entry-count limits are unchanged; the count they check is now always the one the reader goes on to read. Archives no CozyVTT version writes may be answered differently. One whose own comment is longer than 58 bytes is now read, and so is one whose end-of-directory record lists exactly 65,535 entries with no ZIP64 record after them; each was answered 400 "The file is not a ZIP archive, or it is damaged.". These are now answered 400 "The file is not a ZIP archive, or it is damaged." (on a restore with " Nothing was changed." after it) before any entry is unpacked, where they were read: one with bytes after its end-of-directory record and that record's comment; one whose end-of-directory record names a disk other than the first; one whose directory has a record with the wrong signature; one with an extra field whose declared length runs past the record's extra area; one with a record that marks a size or offset 0xFFFFFFFF whose ZIP64 extra field does not carry it; and one with an entry flagged for strong encryption (general purpose bit 6). A restore of the last answered 400 "<name> is encrypted, which an archive from this server never is. Nothing was changed." when bit 0 was set too, and an import did the same when it read that entry. A ZIP64 locator in front of an end-of-directory record whose fields are not full is now always followed: the entry count and directory offset are taken from the ZIP64 record it points to, and the archive is refused as above when that record is bad, or with the entry-count message when its count is over the limit. The old reader used the short record's fields there, so an archive whose ZIP64 record claims 1,000,000 entries and whose short record says 1 is now refused with "The archive lists 1,000,000 files, more than the 1,000 it may hold." on an import, where 1.6.0 read one entry.
- *Campaign archives.* `POST /api/admin/backups/restore` now answers 400 `{ "error": "Invalid Backup", "message": "The archive is damaged, or holds a file name that cannot be written: <name> Nothing was changed." }` for a backup holding a file entry it cannot write: one named as a folder an earlier entry made, one inside a folder named as a file an earlier entry wrote, one with a part of its name longer than the file system allows (255 bytes on most), or one whose name is the folder the backup is unpacked into (such as `./`, holding data). Before, a file named as an earlier folder (`x/y` then `x`), a last part of the name longer than the file system allows, and `./` each ended the backend process. A file inside a file (`x` then `x/y`) and a folder part longer than the file system allows answered 500 "An unexpected error occurred during restore". A folder inside a file (`x` then `x/y/z`) answered 500 `{ "error": "Restore Failed", "message": "The restore could not unpack the backup in the temporary folder (<folder>). Check that it exists and that the backend can write to it. If it is full, free some space on the disk it is on (under Docker, the disk Docker keeps its containers on); on an install without Docker you can also set TMPDIR to a folder with more room. Nothing was changed." }`.
- *Campaign archives.* `POST /api/campaigns/import/preview`, `POST /api/campaigns/import` and `POST /api/admin/backups/restore` now answer 400 "The file is not a ZIP archive, or it is damaged." (on a restore with " Nothing was changed." after it), before any entry is unpacked, for an archive whose directory has an entry whose local header offset plus 30 plus its packed size is more than the file's size, as a ZIP64 extra field claiming an offset or packed size near 2^60 does. Such an offset or size answered 500: on the preview `{ "error": "Preview Failed", "message": "The archive could not be read. The server log has the details." }`, on the import `{ "error": "Import Failed", "message": "The campaign could not be saved, so nothing was imported. The server log has the details." }`, and on a restore "An unexpected error occurred during restore"; a smaller packed size that ran past the end was read on into the rest of the archive. An entry whose local header's name and extra field put its packed bytes past the end of the file is now answered 400 "The archive is damaged: <name> cannot be read." when it is unpacked (a restore adds " Nothing was changed."); it was read as whatever bytes followed, or as empty.
- *Campaign archives.* An archive with an entry name longer than 1,024 bytes (counted in UTF-8) is now refused before any entry is read: `POST /api/campaigns/import/preview` answers 400 `{ "error": "Invalid Archive", "message": "The archive has a file name longer than 1,024 bytes, which an archive from this server never has." }`, `POST /api/campaigns/import` the same message with `"error": "Import Failed"`, and `POST /api/admin/backups/restore` 400 `Invalid Backup` with " Nothing was changed." after it. Such names were read, up to the 65,535 bytes a ZIP name can hold. `POST /api/admin/backups` now leaves out of the backup a file or folder in the uploads folder whose entry name (`uploads/` and its path there) would be longer than 1,024 bytes, logging `The backup left out something in the uploads folder whose name a restore refuses`, as for a name with `..` or a backslash. `GET /api/campaigns/:campaignId/export` names an asset's file in the archive after its format (`.png`, `.jpg`, `.webp`, `.gif`, `.mp3`, `.ogg`, `.wav`, `.pdf`, or nothing) when the part of its original name after the last dot is longer than 20 characters; it took that part whatever its length.
- *Campaign archives.* An entry name holding a NUL character (U+0000) is now refused as an unsafe path: `POST /api/admin/backups/restore` answers 400 `{ "error": "Invalid Backup", "message": "The archive has an unsafe file path, which a backup never has: <name> Nothing was changed." }`, where it answered 500 "An unexpected error occurred during restore", and `POST /api/campaigns/import` answers 400 `Import Failed` "Unsafe file path detected: <name>", where it went on with the import.
- *Campaign archives.* `GET /api/campaigns/:campaignId/export` now writes an asset's `originalName` in `assets/asset-manifest.json` cut to 255 characters, its extension kept when that is 20 characters or fewer, as `POST /api/assets/upload` cuts an upload's name. It wrote the stored name whatever its length, and `POST /api/campaigns/import` skipped an asset whose `originalName` was over 500 characters with the `reason` "Its entry in the archive cannot be read.".
- *Campaign archives.* `POST /api/campaigns/import` imports an archive whose campaign names the value of an unreleased game system as a campaign with no `gameSystem`, and its `warnings` hold "The archive's game system is no longer supported, so the campaign has none."; a creature in the archive naming it is imported with none and the warning `Creature "<name>": its game system is no longer supported, so it was imported with none.`. Both were imported with the value. Any other `gameSystem` the server does not have is reported as before.
- *Campaign archives.* `POST /api/campaigns/import` lists drawings left out of a map's markup layer in `warnings` as "of the GM's share", where it said `of the DM's share`.
- *Campaign archives.* `GET /api/campaigns/:campaignId/export` now writes `gameSystem` as `FIFTH_EDITION`, `FANTASY_D20` or `HORROR_D100` in `manifest.json`, `campaign.json`, each creature in `creatures/creatures.json` and each stat block, and writes `GM`, where it wrote `DM`, as the `audience` of a drawing for the GM alone and as the `share` of each drawing in the GM's share of a map's markup layer. `POST /api/campaigns/import/preview` and `POST /api/campaigns/import` still read the value versions before 1.7.0 gave each system there, and `DM` as a drawing's `audience` or `share`, as the new values (the preview answers the manifest's `gameSystem` with the new value, where it gave the archive's own text), so an archive from any older version imports with its game system, its creatures' systems, its stat blocks and its drawings for the GM alone, with no warning for any of them. An archive 1.7.0 makes imports into 1.6.0 or an older version with no game system and without its drawings for the GM alone.

- *Campaigns.* `POST /api/campaigns` takes `gameSystem` as one of `FIFTH_EDITION`, `FANTASY_D20` and `HORROR_D100`, or the value an older version gave one, or none: the value of an unreleased game system is answered 400 `{ "error": "Validation Error", "message": "CozyVTT does not support that game system." }`, where the campaign was created, and so is any other value it does not take, which was answered "Invalid option: expected one of " and the values it took. `PUT /api/campaigns/:campaignId` answers a value other than the three, or the value an older version gave one, 400 with the same message, where it answered "Invalid game system. Must be one of: " and a list that named the unreleased system. A campaign stored with that value has no `gameSystem` once 1.7.0's migration has run, and takes characters as any campaign with no system does.
- *Campaigns.* Messages that spelled out the role's full name now call it the GM: a GM-only route answers a player or a spectator 403 "This action requires the GM role" (it was `This action requires `, the full name, then ` (DM) role`, with no "the"); `DELETE /api/campaigns/:campaignId/members/:userId` for the sitting GM answers 400 "Cannot remove the GM from the campaign"; `PUT /api/campaigns/:campaignId/members/:userId/role` answers 400 "Cannot change the GM's role" and "Campaign already has a GM. Only one GM per campaign is allowed." (its first sentence gave the full name, and its second was `Only one DM per campaign is allowed.`); `PUT /api/campaigns/:campaignId/dm` answers 400 "That member is already the GM", 200 with `message` "GM role transferred successfully" and 500 "Failed to transfer the GM role". Each of the others said the full name where it now says "GM". The status codes are unchanged. `Role must be DM, PLAYER, or SPECTATOR` is unchanged, since it names the values the route takes.
- *Campaigns.* The campaign invitation email names its sender under "Game master:", and its inbox preview reads "A game master on <instance name> has invited you to their campaign."; both gave the role's full name. When the inviting GM's name cannot be read, the email leaves out the "Game master:" line and opens "You have been invited to join a campaign on <instance name>:", where it gave the role's full name in both places.
- *Campaigns.* Messages that said `DM` now say GM, with the same status codes: `PUT /api/campaigns/:campaignId/gm` (and `.../dm`) answers 403 "Only the GM, the campaign owner, or an admin can transfer the GM role" (it was `Only the DM, the campaign owner, or an admin can transfer the DM role`), and `POST /api/campaigns/:campaignId/leave` from the GM answers 409 "You run this campaign. Hand the GM seat to someone else in the campaign's settings, then leave it." (it said `the DM seat`).
- *Campaigns.* `gameSystem` in every answer that carries a campaign is now `FIFTH_EDITION`, `FANTASY_D20` or `HORROR_D100`, or `null`: 1.7.0's migration renamed each system's stored value, which was the one versions before 1.7.0 used. `POST /api/campaigns` and `PUT /api/campaigns/:campaignId` still take each system's earlier value in `gameSystem` and store the new one.
- *Campaigns.* A membership's `role` is now `GM` for the game master, where it was `DM`, in every answer that carries one: the campaign and its `memberships`, the roster, `userRole` in `GET /api/campaigns/:campaignId/characters`, and the answers of `PUT /api/campaigns/:campaignId/members/:userId/role` and the GM hand-over. The role route still takes `DM` as `role`, read as `GM`, and answers any other value it does not take 400 "Role must be GM, PLAYER, or SPECTATOR" (it was `Role must be DM, PLAYER, or SPECTATOR`).
- *Campaigns.* `PUT /api/campaigns/:campaignId/gm` is new. It hands the GM's seat to another member as `PUT /api/campaigns/:campaignId/dm` does, with the same body, answers and events, and the web client now sends it. `PUT /api/campaigns/:campaignId/dm` still answers.

- *Character data.* `GET /api/characters/templates/FANTASY_D20/blank` now answers with no `classDC` in `data`; it carried `{ "total": 10, "keyAttribute": "strength", "proficiencyRank": "untrained" }`. A Fantasy d20 character made from the blank sheet therefore has no `classDC` until the editor saves one, which it does once a key attribute is chosen; it no longer adds one with `keyAttribute` `intelligence` to a sheet that has none. `classDC` was already optional, and stored sheets are unchanged.
- *Character data.* A `FIFTH_EDITION` sheet takes a new optional field in `data`, `initiativeAdvantage`, true or false, in `POST /api/characters`, `PUT /api/characters/:id`, `POST /api/character-templates` and `PUT /api/character-templates/:id`. It was dropped from the stored sheet before; a value that is not true or false is now answered 400 "Character data does not match game system schema" (for a template, "Template data does not match game system schema"), with `validationErrors` naming the path `initiativeAdvantage`. `GET /api/characters/templates/FIFTH_EDITION/blank` now carries it as `false`. With it `true`, an `initiative.roll` for a token bound to that character rolls `2d20kh1` plus the modifier, where it rolled `1d20` plus the modifier: the `expression` of the `dice.rolled` it sends, and of the roll stored in the dice history, reads like `2d20kh1+2`, and its `breakdown` holds two d20 results with the higher one kept. A sheet with it `true` whose `exhaustionLevel` is 1 or more, or whose `conditions` hold `poisoned` or `exhausted` (in any letter case), still rolls `1d20` plus the modifier, as does a sheet without it or with it `false`.
- *Character data.* `POST /api/characters`, `PUT /api/characters/:id` and `POST /api/character-templates` take `gameSystem` as one of `FIFTH_EDITION`, `FANTASY_D20` and `HORROR_D100`, or the value an older version gave one, or none. The value of an unreleased game system, which they also took, is now answered 400 `{ "error": "Validation Error", "message": "CozyVTT does not support that game system." }`, and so is any other value they do not take, which was answered "Invalid option: expected one of " and the values they took, a list that named that value too. `GET /api/characters/templates/:gameSystem/:templateName` answers a `gameSystem` other than the three, or the value an older version gave one, 400 with the same message, where it answered "Invalid game system: " and the value sent, and the template it served for the unreleased system is gone. `GET /api/character-templates?gameSystem=` answers a value other than the three, the value an older version gave one, and `flexible` 400 with the same message, where it answered "gameSystem must be one of: " and a list that named the unreleased system.
- *Character data.* A character or character template stored with the value of an unreleased game system has no `gameSystem` once 1.7.0's migration has run, and keeps its sheet as stored; it is then read, copied, changed and validated as one with no system, against the Flexible layout. The web client imports a character file naming that value as a character with no `gameSystem` (it refused the file); `POST /api/characters` itself still refuses the value.
- *Character data.* `GET /api/characters/templates/FIFTH_EDITION/blank`, and the same route with any `templateName` other than `fighter`, now answers with `name` "Blank 5E Character" and `description` "A blank character sheet for 5E". Both named the system by its former name.
- *Character data.* `GET /api/characters/templates/FANTASY_D20/blank`, and the same route with any `templateName` other than `fighter`, now answers with `name` "Blank Fantasy d20 Character" and `description` "A blank character sheet for Fantasy d20". Both named the system by its former name.
- *Character data.* The web client's editor for a `HORROR_D100` sheet now saves the rebuilt sheet, and saves a stored sheet's values as they were stored until they are edited: it no longer works `hitPoints.maximum`, `powerPoints.maximum` or `sanity.maximum` out again each time a sheet opens. It sends no sheet while one of `STR`, `CON`, `SIZ`, `INT`, `POW`, `DEX` and `CHA` has no number (the conversion of a sheet from before 1.7.0 that held no characteristics stores none): it names the missing ones and waits for them, and never sends 0 in their place. `wealth.cash` is sent with any fraction it holds. For a sheet that lacks them it adds `hitPoints` and `powerPoints` (maximum and current alike: CON plus SIZ halved and rounded up, and POW), `sanity` (`starting` five times POW and at most 99, `maximum` 100 less the highest `skills.knowledge` rating whose `specialty` is "Blasphemous Lore" and at most 99, `current` the lower of the two), `move` 10 and the `skills` block at its base chances, and to a `skills` block that lacks a skill it adds that skill. When a characteristic is edited it writes `hitPoints.maximum` and `powerPoints.maximum` again, moving `current` by the same amount, and moves `skills.dodge.value`, `skills.gaming.value` and `skills.languageOwn.value` by as much as their base chances moved; when the Blasphemous Lore rating is edited it writes `sanity.maximum` again and lowers `sanity.current` to it. It never sends the formula texts, the half and fifth of a characteristic, a Luck score or a copy of Dodge, which the sheet before 1.7.0 held, and it sends `converted` back as it received it. Passing focus through a box changes nothing it sends: a weapon stored without `skillValue` or `attacks`, a piece of armor without `armorValue` and a `wealth` without `cash` are sent back without them, and a `sanity.current` stored above `sanity.maximum` is sent back as stored. The server is unchanged.
- *Character data.* `GET /api/characters/templates/HORROR_D100/blank`, and the same route with any `templateName` other than `privateinvestigator` or `pi`, now answers with `name` "Blank Horror d100 Character" and `description` "A blank character sheet for Horror d100". Both named the system by its former name.
- *Character data.* `GET /api/characters/templates/FIFTH_EDITION/fighter` now answers a sheet with `background` "Acolyte" (it was "Soldier"); the `insight` and `religion` skills proficient in place of `intimidation` and `survival`, with their `bonus` values to match; `hitDice[0].class` "Fighter" (it was in small letters); 15 `gp` (it was 10); "Dwarvish", "Celestial" and "Elvish" added to `proficienciesAndLanguages`, whose "All shields" is now "Shields"; a third `featuresAndTraits` entry, "Shelter of the Faithful"; the second attack named "Shield" (it was "Shield Bash"); and new `description` text for each feature and new `personality`, `backstory`, `alliesAndOrganizations.name` and `treasure`. Characters made from it before are unchanged.
- *Character data.* `GET /api/characters/templates/FANTASY_D20/blank`, and the same route with any `templateName` other than `fighter`, now answers with `heritage` "Versatile Human" (it was "Versatile Heritage"). `GET /api/characters/templates/FANTASY_D20/fighter` now answers a sheet with `characterName` "Stout Defender" (it was "Dwarven Defender"), `heritage` "Rock Dwarf" (it was "Mountain Dwarf"), and no `alignment` and no `deity`; `feats.ancestryAndHeritage` holding "Unburdened Iron", `feats.class` "Vicious Swing" and `feats.skill` "Intimidating Glare", where the first two held "Darkvision" and "Power Attack" and the third was empty; `classFeatures` "Reactive Strike" (it was "Attack of Opportunity"); the Warhammer strike's `traits` `["Shove"]` and `notes` empty, where its traits also held an ancestry's and its notes named a trait; and new text in every feat's `notes`, each feature's `description` and the sheet's `notes`. Characters made from them before are unchanged.
- *Character data.* `GET /api/characters/templates/HORROR_D100/blank`, and the same route with any `templateName` other than `privateinvestigator` or `pi`, now answers the rebuilt sheet: `characterName` "New Character", `profession` "Student", `era` "Modern", every characteristic 10 (`EDU` 10), `hitPoints` and `powerPoints` 10 of 10, `sanity` `{ "starting": 50, "maximum": 99, "current": 50 }`, `move` 10, every skill at its base chance with `skills.knowledge` holding "Blasphemous Lore" at 0, `wealth.level` "Average", and empty `weapons`, `armor`, `possessions` and `story`. `GET /api/characters/templates/HORROR_D100/privateinvestigator` (and `pi`) now answers with `name` "Private Detective" and the rebuilt sheet of `characterName` "Leo Marchbank", `profession` "Detective": characteristics STR 12, CON 12, SIZ 13, INT 14, POW 12, DEX 14, CHA 11, EDU 13; `hitPoints` 13 of 13, `powerPoints` 12 of 12, `sanity` 60, 99, 60; the skill points the open d100 rules give a starting character spent exactly, 260 on Detective's skills and 140 on others, among them Research 50, Spot 60, Insight 40, `firearm` "Handgun" 50, `drive` "Automobile" 45 and `knowledge` "Law" 35; `weapons` "Medium Revolver (.38)" (`skill` "Firearm (Handgun)", `damage` "1D8", `ammo` 6) and "Fist" (`skill` "Brawl", `damage` "1D3+dm", `range` "Short"); the same `possessions`; `distinctiveFeatures` "A scar above his left eyebrow"; and his story in four titled notes in `story`. Neither carries `converted`. Characters made from them before are converted with every other stored sheet.
